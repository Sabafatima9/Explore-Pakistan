Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often

Pakistan has seen a remarkable improvement in safety and security over the past decade. Popular tourist destinations like Hunza, Skardu, Swat, and Lahore are considered very safe. Millions of domestic and international tourists visit every year. As with any destination, it is advisable to check current travel advisories and register with your embassy.
It depends on the region. For northern areas (Hunza, Skardu, Fairy Meadows), the best time is May to October. For cities like Lahore and Islamabad, October to March is ideal as the weather is cooler. Murree and Nathiagali are popular year-round, with snow in winter. Coastal areas like Gwadar are best visited November to February.
Pakistan offers an e-Visa system for citizens of over 175 countries, making it easy to apply online before travel. Citizens of some countries may also receive a visa on arrival. It is recommended to check the latest requirements on the official Pakistan e-Visa portal (visa.nadra.gov.pk) well in advance of your trip.
Pakistan's currency is the Pakistani Rupee (PKR). ATMs are widely available in cities and larger towns. It is advisable to carry some cash when travelling to remote northern areas where ATM access may be limited. Major hotels in cities accept credit/debit cards, but smaller guesthouses and local shops typically prefer cash.
Urdu is the national language and is spoken and understood throughout the country. English is widely used in business, education, government, and by most educated Pakistanis, so tourists will generally have no difficulty communicating. Regional languages include Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, and Brahui, among many others.
